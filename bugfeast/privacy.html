Bug Feast – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Bug Feast ("the app") is published by Deltalo. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or precise location. The app has no accounts. Your level, coins, language and sound preferences are stored only on your device.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example how many players start or finish a level, app version, device type and country) so we can improve it. Firebase uses an app instance identifier and may process device information, approximate location derived from the IP address and app interactions. This data is not used to identify you personally. Learn more: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Google Play Games Services

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app may submit your progress to a leaderboard. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads for continuing, doubling coins or boosters, occasional full-screen ads between levels, a banner on the level-complete screen and an ad when you return to the app). To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, diagnostics and ad interactions. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Image credits

Level pictures are based on Twemoji graphics by Twitter, Inc and other contributors, licensed under CC-BY 4.0.

Deleting your data

Because game data is stored only on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. Analytics data is retained by Firebase for a limited period and is not linked to your identity.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
