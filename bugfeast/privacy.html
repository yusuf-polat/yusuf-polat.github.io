Bug Feast – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Bug Feast ("the app") is published by Deltalo. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or precise location. You do not need an account to play. Your level, coins, language and sound preferences are stored only on your device. If you choose to link the game to a companion app account, the limited data described under "Optional account linking" is sent to our server.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example how many players start or finish a level, app version, device type and country) so we can improve it. Firebase uses an app instance identifier and may process device information, approximate location derived from the IP address and app interactions. This data is not used to identify you personally. Learn more: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Google Play Games Services

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app may submit your progress to a leaderboard. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional account linking

The app can optionally be linked to your account in a companion app published by Deltalo. Nothing happens unless you start the link yourself from the companion app and confirm it in the game; until then the game sends no data for this feature.

After you confirm, the game sends the following to Deltalo's own server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection:

  • a one-time link code from the companion app, the game's package name and SDK version;
  • a random install identifier created by the game (it is not your advertising ID or any hardware identifier);
  • gameplay progress events such as "level completed", with a random event ID;
  • the number of ads shown and their estimated value as reported by Google AdMob, as daily totals (used only for statistics).

The server stores this together with the companion app account that started the link, and records the IP address of requests for security and fraud prevention. We do not sell this data or share it with third parties. While the account is linked, the app shows more ads than when it is not linked (see Advertising).

You can remove the link at any time from the companion app; clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it also removes the link data on your device. To have the server-side link data deleted, contact us at the address below.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads for continuing, doubling coins or boosters, and full-screen ads between levels. When the game is not linked to a companion app account, fewer full-screen ads are shown and there is no banner or app-open ad. When it is linked, a banner is shown at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads appear more often and an ad may be shown when you return to the app. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, diagnostics and ad interactions. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Deleting your data

Game data is stored on your device, so uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. Analytics data is retained by Firebase for a limited period and is not linked to your identity. To delete account-linking data stored on our server, remove the link in the companion app and email us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
