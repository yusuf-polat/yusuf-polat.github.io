Fart Rafters – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Fart Rafters ("the app") is published by Deltalo. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, coins, unlocked rafts, nickname, language and sound preferences are stored only on your device.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app receives your Play Games player ID and display name to show your name in rooms and to sign you in to our game services. If you choose to allow it on Google's consent screen, the app reads your Play Games friends list (player IDs and display names of friends who also play) so you can invite them. The friends list is shown on your device and is not stored by us.

Online play (Unity Gaming Services)

Multiplayer rooms use Unity Lobby and Relay, and sign-in uses Unity Authentication (anonymous, or linked to Google Play Games). To create and join rooms, Unity processes a player ID, the room code, your nickname and your device's IP address for routing game traffic. Game traffic is relayed through Unity's servers; other players do not see your IP address. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

Invite notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games and allow notifications, the app registers a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token together with your Play Games player ID, nickname and language in our game backend (Unity Cloud Save / Cloud Code), so that friends can send you a room invite notification. We use this only to deliver invites. You can turn off notifications in Android settings at any time. See Firebase privacy information.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (an optional rewarded ad to continue a solo run, occasional full-screen ads between solo runs and a banner on the result screen). To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, diagnostics and ad interactions. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes the data on your device. To delete your invite registration (notification token and Play Games player ID) and your Unity player record, email us from the address below with your in-game nickname; we delete it within 30 days. Registrations of uninstalled apps are also removed automatically when an invite cannot be delivered.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
