English

Short version: open RepCam → Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account and all its data are deleted from our server immediately. If you no longer have the app, e-mail us and we will delete it within 30 days.

1. Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open RepCam and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down to Settings and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation and tap the red Delete button.

Deletion happens immediately on our server. The app then deletes your Firebase sign-in account, signs you out of Google, erases the workout history stored on your phone and returns to the sign-in screen.

2. Delete by e-mail

If you can't use the app (for example you uninstalled it), send an e-mail to polatyusuf1331@gmail.com with the subject "RepCam account deletion" and your nickname (as shown in the league). Send the e-mail from the Google account you sign in with so we can find your account. We process requests within 30 days and reply when it's done.

3. What is deleted

DataWhat happens
NicknameDeleted
Workout scores, personal records, weekly league entries (all weeks)Deleted
Sign-in sessions on all your devicesDeleted (all devices are signed out)
Firebase user ID (pseudonymous sign-in ID) and the Firebase Authentication accountDeleted; signing in with the same Google account later creates a new, empty account
Duel resultsYour side is deleted. Your opponents keep their own match history, in which you appear only as an anonymous, deleted player
Data on your phone (workout history, streaks)Erased by the in-app option; uninstalling the app also removes it

We keep no other copy of your account data after deletion: server backups are deleted automatically after at most 14 days, so any remaining copy disappears within that time. Short-lived technical server logs (such as IP address and timestamps, kept for security) expire on their own.

See also the privacy policy.

Türkçe

Kısaca: RepCam'i aç → Profil → Ayarlar → Hesabı sil ve onayla. Hesabın ve tüm verileri sunucumuzdan hemen silinir. Uygulama artık sende yoksa bize e-posta gönder, 30 gün içinde sileriz.

1. Uygulama içinden sil (önerilen)

  1. RepCam'i aç ve Profil sekmesine git.
  2. Ayarlar bölümüne in ve Hesabı sil'e dokun.
  3. Onay metnini oku ve kırmızı Sil düğmesine dokun.

Silme işlemi sunucumuzda hemen gerçekleşir. Ardından uygulama Firebase giriş hesabını siler, Google hesabından çıkış yapar, telefonundaki antrenman geçmişini siler ve giriş ekranına döner.

2. E-postayla sil

Uygulamayı kullanamıyorsan (örneğin kaldırdıysan) polatyusuf1331@gmail.com adresine "RepCam hesap silme" konulu, takma adını (ligde göründüğü gibi) içeren bir e-posta gönder. Hesabını bulabilmemiz için e-postayı giriş yaptığın Google hesabından gönder. Talepleri 30 gün içinde işleriz ve tamamlandığında yanıt veririz.
