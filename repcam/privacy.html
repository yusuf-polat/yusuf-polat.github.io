RepCam – Privacy Policy

Publisher: Deltalo · App: RepCam (com.deltalo.repcam) · Effective date: 16 November 2026

English

Short version: your camera video is processed only on your phone and is never recorded, stored or uploaded. You sign in with your Google account through Google Firebase; our own server stores only a pseudonymous Firebase user ID, your nickname and your scores. During a live VS duel only body pose points (a "skeleton") are sent. We use Google Firebase for sign-in, app analytics (with your consent where required), crash reports and performance measurements, and Google AdMob for ads.

1. Who we are

RepCam is published by Deltalo ("we", "us"). Contact: polatyusuf1331@gmail.com.

2. Camera

RepCam uses the camera to detect your body pose and count repetitions. Camera frames are analysed in real time on your device by an on-device pose model. Frames are never recorded, saved, or uploaded to us or anyone else.

3. Sign-in (Google account via Firebase Authentication)

An account is required so that your scores, league rank and duels are kept and can be restored on another phone. You sign in with your Google account. Sign-in is handled by Google Sign-In and Google Firebase Authentication: Google/Firebase process your Google account's e-mail address, name and profile picture to authenticate you and create a Firebase user ID. Our server does not receive or store your e-mail address, name or photo; it verifies the sign-in token issued by Firebase and stores only the pseudonymous Firebase user ID. See the Firebase privacy and security information and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. VS duels

In a live push-up duel, the app sends the pose keypoints (about 33 body point coordinates per frame, no images) and your rep count to our server, which relays them to your opponent for the duration of the match. Keypoints are used only to run the match and are not kept after the match ends. The match result (win/loss/draw and rating) is stored with your account.

6. Analytics, crash reports and performance (Google Firebase)

  • Google Analytics for Firebase collects app interactions (for example: screens viewed, workout started/completed with exercise type, rep count and duration, duel started/finished, language and sensitivity settings), device information, the app instance ID and advertising ID, and approximate location derived from the IP address. We use it to understand how the app is used and to improve it. We do not send your name, e-mail, nickname or other directly identifying data to Analytics. Where consent is required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Analytics only collects data after you give consent in Google's consent tool, and follows your choices; you can change them in the app under Profile → Settings → Privacy options.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs and diagnostic information (stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, app state at the time of the crash, and the pseudonymous Firebase user ID) so that we can fix bugs.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring collects performance data (app start time, screen rendering, network request timings to our server, camera start time) together with device and app information.
  • Firebase Remote Config downloads app settings (for example ad frequency limits); it uses the app instance ID.

Firebase services are provided by Google, which acts as our data processor for this data. Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months; Crashlytics crash data for up to 90 days.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

RepCam is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, and app interaction/diagnostic data to show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent through Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice in the app's privacy settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your nickname, scores and pose keypoints (during a match) are visible to other players as described above. Data is otherwise shared only with service providers that run the service (hosting; Google for Firebase Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Remote Config, Google Sign-In and AdMob) or when required by law.

9. Security

Data between the app, our server and Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

10. Retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data (Firebase user ID, nickname, scores, records, league entries, duel results, sessions) at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Deletion on our server is immediate; the app also deletes your Firebase Authentication user and erases the workout history stored on your phone. In duels, your opponents keep their own match history, where you remain only as an anonymous deleted player. Alternatively, send an e-mail to polatyusuf1331@gmail.com with your nickname and we complete deletion within 30 days. Details: how to delete your account. Server backups are deleted automatically after at most 14 days. Sign-in sessions on our server expire after 180 days without use. Analytics and Crashlytics data is kept for the periods in section 6 and is not linked to your name or e-mail. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored locally on your device.

11. Children

RepCam is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Contact us at the e-mail above.

13. Health notice

RepCam is not a medical device. Rep counts and calorie values are estimates.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app.

6. Analitik, kilitlenme raporları ve performans (Google Firebase)

  • Google Analytics for Firebase; uygulama etkileşimlerini (örneğin görüntülenen ekranlar, egzersiz türü, tekrar sayısı ve süreyle başlatılan/tamamlanan antrenman, başlayan/biten düello, dil ve hassasiyet ayarları), cihaz bilgilerini, uygulama örneği kimliğini ve reklam kimliğini, IP adresinden türetilen yaklaşık konumu toplar. Uygulamanın nasıl kullanıldığını anlamak ve geliştirmek için kullanırız. Analytics'e adını, e-postanı, takma adını veya seni doğrudan tanımlayan başka veri göndermeyiz. İznin gerektiği yerlerde (örneğin AEA, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre) Analytics yalnızca Google'ın onay aracında izin verdikten sonra veri toplar ve tercihlerine uyar; tercihlerini Profil → Ayarlar → Gizlilik seçenekleri'nden değiştirebilirsin.
  • Firebase Crashlytics; hataları düzeltebilmemiz için kilitlenme kayıtlarını ve teşhis bilgilerini (yığın izi, cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi ve uygulama sürümü, kilitlenme anındaki uygulama durumu ve takma isimli Firebase kullanıcı kimliği) toplar.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring; performans verilerini (uygulama açılış süresi, ekran çizimi, sunucumuza yapılan ağ isteklerinin süreleri, kameranın açılma süresi) cihaz ve uygulama bilgileriyle birlikte toplar.
  • Firebase Remote Config; uygulama ayarlarını (örneğin reklam sıklığı sınırları) indirir, uygulama örneği kimliğini kullanır.

Firebase hizmetleri Google tarafından sağlanır; Google bu veriler için veri işleyenimiz olarak hareket eder. Analytics verileri en fazla 14 ay, Crashlytics kilitlenme verileri en fazla 90 gün saklanır.
