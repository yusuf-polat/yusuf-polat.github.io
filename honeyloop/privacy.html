Honey Loop: Bee Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Honey Loop ("the app") is published by Deltalo. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or location. Your game progress, lives and sound preferences are stored only on your device.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example how many games or levels are started and finished, app version, device type and country) so we can improve it. Firebase uses an app instance identifier and may process device information, approximate location derived from the IP address and app interactions. This data is not used to identify you personally. Learn more: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Optional account linking

The app can optionally be linked to your account in a companion app published by Deltalo. Nothing happens unless you start the link yourself from the companion app and confirm it in the game; until then the game sends no data for this feature.

After you confirm, the game sends the following to Deltalo's own server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection: a one-time link code, the game's package name and SDK version; a random install identifier created by the game (not your advertising ID or any hardware identifier); gameplay progress events such as "level completed", with a random event ID; and the number of ads shown and their estimated value as reported by Google AdMob, as daily totals (statistics only). The server stores this together with the companion app account that started the link and records the IP address of requests for security and fraud prevention. We do not sell this data or share it with third parties. You can remove the link at any time from the companion app; clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it removes the link data on your device. To have the server-side link data deleted, email us at the address below.

While the account is linked, the app shows more ads than when it is not linked (see Advertising).

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button inside the app. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

When the game is not linked to a companion app account, fewer full-screen ads are shown and there is no banner. When it is linked, a banner is shown at the bottom of the screen and full-screen ads appear more often. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
