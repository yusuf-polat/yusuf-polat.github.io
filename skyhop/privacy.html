Skyhop: Island Jump – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Skyhop ("the app") is published by Deltalo. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or location. Your best score and sound preference are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button inside the app. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
