00:17 Son Kayıt – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

00:17 Son Kayıt ("the app") is published by Deltalo. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or precise location. The app has no accounts of its own. Your progress (current night, found story records, anomalies reported), language, sound and control preferences are stored only on your device.

No advertising

The app does not show ads and does not contain an advertising SDK. It does not use your advertising ID.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example how many players start or finish a night, how many anomalies are reported, app version, device type and country) so we can improve it. Firebase uses an app instance identifier and may process device information, approximate location derived from the IP address and app interactions. This data is not used to identify you personally. Learn more: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the app uses it for achievements and a leaderboard. Google processes your Play Games profile and the scores and achievements you earn according to the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

Optional account linking

The app can optionally be linked to your account in a companion app published by Deltalo. Nothing happens unless you start the link yourself from the companion app and confirm it in the game; until then the game sends no data for this feature.

After you confirm, the game sends the following to Deltalo's own server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection: a one-time link code, the game's package name and SDK version; a random install identifier created by the game (not an advertising ID or any hardware identifier); and gameplay progress events such as "night completed" or "anomaly reported", with a random event ID. The server stores this together with the companion app account that started the link and records the IP address of requests for security and fraud prevention. We do not sell this data or share it with third parties. You can remove the link at any time from the companion app; clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it removes the link data on your device. To have the server-side link data deleted, email us at the address below.

Deleting your data

Because game data is stored only on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. Analytics data is retained by Firebase for a limited period and is not linked to your identity.

Children

The app is a psychological horror game, is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
