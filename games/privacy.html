DeltaLo browser games – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06. This policy covers the Chrome extensions Neon Drift Highway, Skyline Stack Tower and Marble Skies Run, published by DeltaLo.

Data we collect

The extensions do not collect, transmit or sell personal data. Scores, coins, unlocked items and sound settings are stored only in your browser. The extensions contain no ads, analytics or tracking.

Permissions

The storage permission is used only to remember, on your device, that the game's online page has already been opened once.

Online game page

The first time you click the extension icon, the game's page on GameDistribution opens once in a new tab. That website is run by a third party, shows ads and has its own privacy policy.
